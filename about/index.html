About

Welcome to my blog! Here, you will learn about the blog and what it offers.

Who am I?

I am Awiteb, a university student specializing in Public Relations, a contributor to Free and Open Source Software, and a lover of the Rust programming language.

Content Provided

Here, I will write about what’s on my mind and try to make it valuable. Most often, what’s on my mind relates to software, the Rust language and Nostr.

How the blog was built

This blog is open source; you can access its code here: git.4rs.nl/awiteb/blog. This means you can build your own blog today after forking it. The blog uses the static site generator Zola and the Tabi theme.

Where it’s hosted

It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub for hosting websites that do not require a server to run.

Ways to contact me

The method of communication you should use to contact me depends on the reason for contact. Here are the methods, and you should choose the appropriate one based on your reason for reaching out.

Blog Comments

Most posts has a comment section at the bottom. You should use it if the reason for contacting me is an inquiry related specifically to that post and doesn’t go beyond its scope.

Email

Contact me via email if your topic does not require chatting and can be answered with a single message. You will find my email address at the bottom of all blog pages. You will also find my PGP key, and it is important that you use it when sending me a message. Learn more.

Telegram / Matrix / Nostr (NIP-17)

Contact me via the Telegram, Matrix, or Nostr if your topic requires chatting and cannot be answered with a single message. You will find my accounts on Matrix, Telegram, and Nostr at the bottom of all blog pages.
